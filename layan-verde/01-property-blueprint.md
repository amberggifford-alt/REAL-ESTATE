# Layan Verde, Phuket: Property Blueprint

A production blueprint of the Layan Verde development, written so a shot list and video edit can be built on it. It records what public sources say about the site, buildings, unit types and amenities, and how sure we are of each fact.

> **Research note.** The official site (layanverde.com) and most listing sites could not be opened from the research environment, so this blueprint is built from search-result summaries of the developer site, the Dusit press release, news coverage and broker listings (sources at the end). Where sources disagree, both figures are shown. **Before anything is published, check each item marked ⚠ against the developer's current brochure and official floor plans.**

## 2. Masterplan concept: what the camera should show

1. **"See but not be seen."** The mid-rise blocks are placed so residents get views without looking into each other's homes. *Camera:* angles between buildings that show how they are offset, and views out from terraces.
2. **Lifted buildings, car-free green ground.** Buildings sit raised up, with amenities and back-of-house underneath. The ground level stays a car-free landscape. *Camera:* low glides through the gardens and tilt-ups from the undercroft.
3. **Water through the landscape.** Waterfalls, streams and ponds run through the gardens. *Camera:* macro water texture and rack focus to the architecture.
4. **Two tiers plus hotels.** Luxury blocks, Premium blocks and the Dusit hotel zone. *Camera and graphics:* colour-coded callouts on the masterplan render.

## 5. Production reality check

- **The project is under construction (completion 2027–2028).** Amenities and finished interiors don't exist yet. Each shot in the shot list has a `source`:
  - **Film now**: coastline, beaches, hills, construction progress, sales gallery and show unit.
  - **Show unit / Render**: film the show unit if it matches the unit type, otherwise use the developer's official renders.
  - **Render/CGI** and **Render/AI**: get developer-approved renders, or generate AI shots from the official renders and label them as artist impressions.
  - **Graphic**: motion graphics such as maps, floor plans and fact cards.
- **Permissions:** permission from the developer to use renders, floor plans, logos and the Dusit mark. Drone needs CAAT and NBTC registration. Model releases for talent. Guardian releases for any identifiable children.
- **Light:** the site faces the Andaman Sea to the west, so sunset is the hero light (around 18:15–18:30 in Nov–Feb). Sunrise suits the beach and gardens. The dry season (Nov–Apr) has the clearest skies.
- **Disclaimers on every cut:** "Images are artist impressions. Specifications, sizes and completion dates are subject to change."
